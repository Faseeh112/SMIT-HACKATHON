# About Saylani Welfare International

Saylani Welfare International is a Pakistani welfare organization that
provides services in education, healthcare, food distribution, and
skills training across Pakistan.

## Saylani Mass IT Training (SMIT)

SMIT offers free IT courses including web development, mobile app
development, graphic design, and digital marketing. Courses are open to
applicants generally between 15 and 45 years old. Classes run in
multiple cities including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and Rawalpindi.
Admission is via an online registration form followed by an aptitude
test. There is no tuition fee for SMIT courses.

## Admission Process (General)

To apply for a Saylani program, applicants typically need:
1. A valid CNIC or B-Form (for minors)
2. Two passport-size photographs
3. Proof of residence
4. A completed application form (available online or at the nearest
   Saylani center)

Admission decisions are usually communicated within 1-2 weeks of
completing the aptitude test where one is required.

## Welfare Programs

Saylani runs a monthly ration support program for eligible low-income
households, medical assistance for those who cannot afford treatment,
and an emergency food (Dastarkhwan) program that provides free meals at
various centers.
