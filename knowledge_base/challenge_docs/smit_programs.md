# Saylani Mass IT Training (SMIT) — Program Details

Source note: compiled from Saylani Welfare's official pages
(saylaniwelfare.com/services/education/technical-education/saylani-mass-it-training
and saylanimit.com) as of Sep 2026. For exact current fees (free unless
stated otherwise), start dates, and seat availability, always confirm via

course line-ups and campus locations change over time.

## What SMIT is
Saylani Mass IT Training (SMIT) is Saylani Welfare's flagship free
technology-education program, launched in 2013, aimed at giving Pakistani
youth industry-relevant digital skills at no cost. Most courses require
only basic computer literacy to enroll.

## Course categories offered
SMIT publishes over 80 specialized courses across areas including:
- Web development and mobile app development
- Networking / CCNA certification and cybersecurity (including CyberOps-track content)
- Graphic design
- Computer-based accounting
- Freelancing-oriented courses (e.g. video editing, graphic design for freelancers)
- Other IT/vocational tracks added periodically — check saylanimit.com/courses for the current list

## Campuses
SMIT operates campuses in multiple Pakistani cities (Karachi, Hyderabad,
Faisalabad, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and others) — use
saylanimit.com/campuses (campus locator by city) or ask SahulatAI to
search for a specific city's campus details.

## Admission process
1. Apply online via the enrollment form at saylanimit.com/enroll (or
   through saylaniwelfare.com's admission form).
2. Sit an online entry test / aptitude test for the chosen course.
3. Results/roll numbers and interview calls (where applicable) are
   announced on SMIT's official channels.
General eligibility (per the organization's own materials): applicants
roughly 15-45 years old, basic computer literacy, no income requirement
for SMIT specifically (unlike some of Saylani's welfare programs, which
do have income-based eligibility).

## Getting help
- General/course questions: saylanimit.com/chat (SMIT's own site chatbot) or saylanimit.com/contact
- Anything not resolved online: Saylani Welfare Head Office UAN 111-729-526
