# Eligibility Notes (Challenge Document)

Ration support is generally available to households in any city whose
verified monthly household income is at or below the threshold set by
the welfare program committee. Applicants must provide proof of income
and residence during verification.

SMIT IT courses are open to applicants aged 15 to 45. There is no income
requirement for SMIT courses.

Medical assistance eligibility is assessed case by case at the nearest
Saylani welfare center; there is no fixed income cutoff published for
this program.
